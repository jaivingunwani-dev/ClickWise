================================================================================
                         THE KEY CONCEPT
================================================================================

How Anthropic SDK Works:

YOUR CONFIG:    https://api.anthropic.com
SDK APPENDS:    /v1/messages
FULL URL:       https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages

CORRECT: https://api.anthropic.com (without /v1)
WRONG:   https://api.anthropic.com/v1 (with /v1)

If you include /v1, SDK creates:
https://api.anthropic.com/v1/v1/messages → 404 ERROR
